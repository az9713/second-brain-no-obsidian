# Dream Sequence

## Summary

Dream sequence is the scheduled or manually invoked maintenance routine for this second brain. It ingests new raw files, folds knowledge into the wiki, runs lint, repairs the index, updates registries, appends to the log, and writes a report.

## Source-Backed Claims

- Jay describes dream sequence as a scheduled or manual routine.
- Jay says it ingests new raw files and then runs a lint sequence.
- Jay names lint checks including contradictions, stale claims, duplicates, and orphans.

## Local Operating Policy

This vault implements dream sequence in seven phases:

1. Raw ingestion.
2. Wiki folding.
3. Lint sequence.
4. Index repair.
5. Processed registry update.
6. Log update.
7. Dream report.
