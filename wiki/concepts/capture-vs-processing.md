# Capture vs Processing

## Summary

Capture is fast intake into `raw/`. Processing is deliberate synthesis into `wiki/`.

## Source-Backed Claims

- Jay shows sources being saved into the raw folder.
- Jay shows the system can update the wiki immediately after ingest.
- Jay also shows changing the policy so new raw files do not auto-update the wiki and are handled by dream sequence later.

## Local Operating Policy

This vault defaults to capture-only. The wiki is updated only when:

- the user says `process now`; or
- `/dream-sequence` runs.

## Interpretation

Capture-only keeps source intake low-friction and reduces one-source over-synthesis. Batch processing gives the dream sequence a better chance to compare sources, detect contradictions, and fold knowledge into existing pages.

Graphify-style exports make this distinction sharper: the generated Markdown graph may look like wiki content, but it is still processing output that needs quarantine, source wiring, and review before promotion into durable wiki pages.
