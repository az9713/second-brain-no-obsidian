# Knowledge-Base Lint

## Summary

Knowledge-base lint is a maintenance pass that checks the wiki for structural, factual, and retrieval-quality defects.

## Source-Backed Claims

- Jay explicitly mentions a lint sequence inside dream sequence.
- The transcript describes lint as checking contradictions, stale claims, duplicates, and orphans.

## Implementation Recommendations

This vault makes lint first-class through:

- [Lint Report](../lint.md)
- [Contradictions](../contradictions.md)
- [Stale Claims](../stale-claims.md)
- [Duplicate Candidates](../duplicate-candidates.md)
- [Orphaned Pages](../orphaned-pages.md)
- [Broken Links](../broken-links.md)

Lint should also check missing provenance and retrieval defects such as vague page titles, weak summaries, missing aliases, and overlong pages.

## Interpretation

Without lint, the wiki can become a polished notes folder. With lint, it can become a self-correcting research substrate.
